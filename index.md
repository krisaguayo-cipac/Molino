# Welcome to Molino
"Molino" means mill in Spanish - an ancient technology independently developed by multiples cultures all over the world. In the recent centuries, Molino technology has transformed, allowing processing of raw resources at scale, and therefore consumption of earth's resources at scale. 

The unprecedented rate of consumption is preceded by the forethought: The earth is there to be consumed. It is not a universally recognized however that the earth is to be consumed. Contested by alternative ways of knowing, the dominant narrative of the past few centuries has failed us, large scale consumption has destabilized earth systems and today the Northern Atlantic Ocean current, which pushes cold water into the south, and instigates  the movement of water across the earth, is weakening. 

Importantly, Mills are not inherently destructive. Across the centuries, they have been used to make food widely available. Instead, their impacts are mitigated by their users. At their dawn, mills belong to their communities, no one individual claiming full ownership. Over thousands of years, this practice has shifted to today, where individuals may own Mills, and rent out their use to community members. 

The application of mills through ownership, mirrors the previously mentioned framework of "the earth is to be consumed". It both perspectives, there is a difference between having and not having, processed, and not processing, destroying and not destroying. This essential function of the Mill, of controlling the condition of materials across its complimentary states is a key characteristic of Molinos. 

This Molino is digital and it does not process grain. Instead, this molino processes what has not be processed.
